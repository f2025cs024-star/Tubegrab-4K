Setup In 3 Simple Steps

No installer wizards, no account creation, and zero background telemetry.

01

Extract the Suite

Unzip tubegrab-windows-full.zip and run install.bat. It configures Python and registers the background helper automatically.

02

Load Extension

In Chrome or Edge, go to chrome://extensions, toggle Developer mode ON, and click Load unpacked on the extension folder.

03

Download Directly

Open any video on YouTube. Select your preferred resolution from the quality dropdown right beside the video title and click Download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TubeGrab.

Why does TubeGrab use a desktop helper?

YouTube streams 1080p, 1440p, and 4K video separate from audio. A standard browser extension cannot merge high-bitrate video and audio streams at full speed without freezing the tab. TubeGrab's tiny helper merges them instantly using FFmpeg.

Does the desktop helper run in the background forever?

No. TubeGrab uses an intelligent auto-shutdown timer. 5 seconds after your downloads finish, the helper completely terminates itself, leaving 0% CPU and 0 MB RAM usage.

How do I update TubeGrab when YouTube changes?

Inside your Windows Suite folder, simply double-click update.bat. It pulls the latest YouTube extraction algorithms and engine scripts in 3 seconds.

Is TubeGrab safe and free?

Yes. TubeGrab is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License. It does not collect cookies, browsing data, or telemetry.
