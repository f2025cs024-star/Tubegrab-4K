Frequently Asked Questions

Why does StreamForge use a desktop helper?

YouTube streams 1080p, 1440p, and 4K video separately from audio. Standard browser extensions cannot merge them without freezing. The StreamForge helper muxes them losslessly via FFmpeg.

How do I update StreamForge?

Inside your Windows Suite folder, simply double-click update.bat. It pulls the latest YouTube extraction algorithms in 3 seconds.

Is it completely free?

Yes. StreamForge is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License with no ads, tracking, or account requirements.
