🚀 Quick 3-Step Setup Guide

1

Load in Chrome

Download & unzip the repo. Go to chrome://extensions, toggle Developer mode on, and click Load unpacked selecting the downloaded folder.

2

Start Desktop 4K Engine

Run launch_idm_4k.bat on your PC. This unlocks gigabit 4K/8K stream extraction and FFmpeg video+audio muxing with zero timeouts.

3

Enjoy 4K Downloads

Open any YouTube video! Click the sleek TubeGrab 4K capsule button next to the Subscribe button to download any quality from 144p to 4K or MP3.
